Transportation

  • Maxi taxis and buses are quite affordable and are found throughout Trinidad especially in major towns like Port of Spain, San Fernando, Arima, Chaguanas, and Sangre Grande.
  • If you have a vehicle, parking is available most of the time but on weekends they are quite busy.
  • Renting a car is also an option.

Travel Essentials

  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+) and Sunglasses
  • Hat or Cap
  • Walking shoes, Watershoes or Sandals
  • Lightweight Clothing
  • Swimsuit and Towel
  • Insect repellent
  • Bring plenty of water!!
  • Hand Sanitizer
  • Phone or Camera
  • Portable Charger
  • Backpack or Totebag

Safety Tips

  • Stay in groups in isolated areas
  • Always have you vehicle locked
  • Avoid leaving valuables inside.
  • Follow the local signs.
  • Be aware of your things and your environment.
