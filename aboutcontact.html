Our Story

RoamChaguaramas was founded in 2023 by a group of young developers from Chaguaramas, Trinidad. They were frustrated by the lack of clear information about any events, hikes, or attractions in the area so they decided to build RoamChaguaramas, an all-in-one platform to help promote local tourism and small businesses.

Mission

We want to provide locals and tourists with up-to-date information about Chaguaramas' top attractions, and we also want to show off the natural beauty of our land.

Team

Our team is now made up of dedicated developers and local enthusiasts working together to make the website its fullest potential of making it easy and fun for everyone to explore Chaguaramas.
